Drops and orders land at the crosshair. Pick the Ship Gun on the bar and hold CALL to free-fire. Tap ARMORY to upgrade mid-mission.
  • Four Helldivers, one per group, four stratagems each. They push on and clear bases, the citadel and the map by themselves. You never steer them.
  • You run the sky: scan, direct SEAF, hold the line. Watch for assault warnings, find the force with a UAV, then set a line or a ring and have strikes ready.
  • Orbital calls land inside the strike zone. The Ship Gun free-fires anywhere you can see. Your fire hurts friendlies, the gun does not.
  • SEAF arrive by troop pelican, in squads you can split, merge and draw plays for. Medics heal everyone. Spotters call Eagle fire missions.
  • Kills, bases, points of interest, breaches and objectives pay credits. Spend them in the armory.
  • Only failure ends a session. Keep the squad alive.
