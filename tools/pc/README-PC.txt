ORBITAL FIRE SUPPORT - PC VERSION
Super Earth Special Forces fan project (unofficial; no game assets, text or audio)

HOW TO PLAY
1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere (do not run it from inside the zip).
2. Windows: double-click Play-Windows.bat
   Mac:     double-click Play-Mac.command
   Linux:   run ./Play-Linux.sh
   Any system: just double-click Orbital-Fire-Support.html and it opens in your browser.
3. Press PLAY, wait for the loading bar, then click CONTINUE.

It is one self-contained file. No install, no internet, no server.

NOTES
- Use Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari from the last couple of years.
- Press F11 for full screen. Sound starts after your first click.
- Your personal best and an unfinished session are saved in the browser's local storage on this PC.
  If you open the game in a different browser, it starts fresh there.
- Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" for the .bat file because it is not signed.
  Choose More info, then Run anyway. It is plain text; open it in Notepad to read it.
  macOS may need right-click, Open the first time. If it says permission denied, run: chmod +x Play-Mac.command
- Scores are posted by hand: at the end of a session press COPY and paste the score block into your community channel.

CONTROLS (mouse and keyboard)
Mouse aim, click fire (hold for the Gatling), 1-6 or Q/E weapon (7 is the nuke once a launch site is armed), R reload
WASD or right-drag pan, wheel zoom, Space lock camera on the squad
F approve a fire mission request
G SEAF drop, H Helldiver drop (inside a living Helldiver's range), V SEAF move, C SEAF escort (they land at the cursor)
B turrets: click one to select it, then click the ground to give it a focus point

THE SQUAD
The briefing shows the mission objective and the 12 stratagems the Helldivers will call on their own.
They do the objective first; clearing bases and pulling out are bonus points. 30 minutes per mission.
T white-hot / black-hot, Esc pause
