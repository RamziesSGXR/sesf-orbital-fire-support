ORBITAL FURY
Super Earth Special Forces fan project (unofficial; no game assets, text or audio)

Start it from the Start menu or the desktop: "Orbital Fury". It opens in its own window and needs no internet.

- Touch screens work: tap the cards and buttons, drag the map with one finger, pinch to zoom, hold FIRE to shoot.
  With a mouse or trackpad the controls switch back on the first click.
- F11 or the window's maximise button for full screen. Sound starts after your first tap or click.
- Your progress and settings are kept between sessions.
- To remove it: Settings > Apps > Orbital Fury > Uninstall.

The game is the single file Orbital-Fury.html in this folder; it also opens in any modern browser by itself.
